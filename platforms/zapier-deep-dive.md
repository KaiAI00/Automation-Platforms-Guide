## ⚠️ Critical Scaling Bottlenecks (The Cost Wall)

While Zapier is phenomenal for early-stage MVPs, scaling business processes on the platform introduces strict operational limits:

1. **Exponential Task Cost:** Multi-step "Zaps" consume a task fee at every single step. A single lead submission passing through 5 steps will burn 5 tasks.
2. **Brittle Complex Logic:** Building complex nesting paths or conditional routing inside Zapier leads to heavily cluttered interfaces that are hard to debug.
3. **Data Lock-In:** You cannot easily export your automated logic formulas or self-host your setups if cloud-hosting prices increase.
